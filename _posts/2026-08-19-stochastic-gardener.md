I just started a PhD in Computer Science at [Texas State University](https://www.txst.edu/) and this will be my
blog post as I journey towards a PhD. My research advisor is [Dr Aniruddha Bora](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aniruddha-bora-49b73a80/) and I will be working in the [SPARKS Research Lab](https://aniruddhabora.github.io/spark/),
which develops next-generation AI methods that are grounded in scientific principles. 

This is an exciting time in AI, where we are starting to see a highly connected world of drones, 
autonomous cars and [Physical AI](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/generative-physical-ai/) in all phases of our lives. I think this new world will be highly dependent on algorithms that don’t just fit data, but respect 
the laws of physics, scale to real-world complexity, and provide interpretable insights for scientific 
discovery. This area encompasses many forms such as:

+ Physics-Informed Neural Networks
+ Neural Operators
+ Hybrid Modeling
+ Physical AI application uses

So when I learn something new or find something cool, I'm going to blog about it, should be fun!
