My first blog! A little bit about myself, currently I'm a Data Scientist in Austin, TX. In my job, I gain insights and develop scalable data
driven products. Before that I was a Quantitative Developer in the Energy industry. A Quantitative Developer or Quant is someone who codes and
develops pricing models and algorithms. I'm been very blessed and fortunate working in both of these very interesting areas (Quant, Data Science) 
and I think it gives me a unique perspective. So when I was thinking on what to write in a blog, why not combine methods from both of these disciplines for
test driven model development? 

This is an exciting time in model development, where we are starting to see a highly connected world of drones, self driving cars, [IOT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_of_things) 
and machine based recommendations, in all phases of our lives. This new world will be highly dependent on **production machine intelligence models**, to deliver these services. Maybe 
we can gain insights from the past as we develop these models in this new revolution? In **Test Driven Intelligence** I'm going to blog about machine intelligence model development 
from my background in the derivative industry, should be fun!
